Payments & M-Pesa

Affordable Rentals processes rent payments through Safaricom's M-Pesa Daraja platform. By making a payment you agree to the following:

  • All payments are processed in Kenyan Shillings (KES)
  • Payment amounts are set by your landlord and cannot be altered by Affordable Rentals
  • Once a payment is confirmed by Safaricom, it is final and non-refundable unless the landlord explicitly authorises a refund
  • Affordable Rentals is not responsible for failed transactions caused by insufficient M-Pesa balance, network issues or Safaricom service outages
  • Your M-Pesa PIN is never stored or seen by Affordable Rentals — all PIN entry happens directly on Safaricom's secure STK Push interface
💡 Always verify the payment amount before entering your M-Pesa PIN. Contact your landlord immediately if you suspect an incorrect charge.
